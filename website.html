// 01 — About

Building Minds.
Building Machines.

NeuraCET is the official Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning club of College of Engineering Trivandrum. We're a community of passionate students exploring the bleeding edge of AI research, practical ML applications, and data-driven problem solving.

From hands-on workshops to hackathons, from paper reading sessions to industry talks — we create opportunities for students to grow, collaborate, and push the boundaries of what's possible.

What We Do

Workshop
ML Bootcamp

Hands-on intensive covering supervised learning, neural networks, and model deployment using Python and PyTorch.

// UPCOMING · STAY TUNED
Hackathon
NeuraHack

24-hour AI-focused hackathon open to all CET students. Build something that matters, compete for glory.

// ANNUAL EVENT
Seminar
Paper Reading Club

Weekly deep dives into landmark AI research papers — from Attention Is All You Need to the latest LLM breakthroughs.

// EVERY WEEK

Group sessions to tackle Kaggle challenges together — learn collaborative ML workflows and competitive data science.
